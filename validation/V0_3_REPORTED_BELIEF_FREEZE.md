# v0.3 reported-belief/action synthetic evidence freeze

Date: 2026-08-25

## Question

Can an update rule be identified when the agent's internal belief state is latent and the experimenter observes only noisy probability reports, binary actions, or both?

## Generative baseline

The synthetic chain is now

`world -> raw evidence -> corrupted evidence -> latent belief -> report/action`.

The received binary evidence sequence is supplied to every candidate update model. Internal beliefs are not supplied to model comparison. Each model must reconstruct its own latent trajectory, which is then scored through an observation likelihood.

Candidate families are:

- standard Bayes,
- leaky Bayes,
- anchored Bayes,
- full PCC-tempered updating.

Leaky Bayes is not mathematically independent of PCC: it is the `Pressure=1` slice of the PCC family. The implementation keeps it as a named comparison baseline while documenting this nesting explicitly.

## Observation models

Reported probabilities are generated by Gaussian noise in binary log-odds space. Binary actions are generated from a soft log-odds decision policy.

For model comparison, uniform priors are placed over each supplied parameter grid. Model evidence is the average likelihood over the grid rather than the maximum likelihood alone, so a larger flexible model is penalized when most of its parameter settings fit poorly.

## Frozen synthetic run

Generator:

- Pressure = 1.5
- Control = 0.6
- Chaos = 0.10
- 80 updates
- report noise sigma = 0.15 in log-odds space
- action inverse temperature beta = 2.0

Results from `experiments/10_report_action_model_comparison.py`:

### Noisy reports

PCC is recovered decisively. The best PCC grid point is the generating `Pressure=1.5, Control=0.6` setting.

### Actions only

Identification is much weaker. PCC receives posterior model probability about 0.56 and leaky Bayes about 0.40. The best PCC pressure shifts to 1.25 while Control remains 0.6.

### Reports plus actions

PCC is again recovered decisively, with the correct `Pressure=1.5, Control=0.6` grid point.

## Interpretation

This establishes a useful synthetic distinction between measurement channels. Noisy probability reports can retain enough trajectory information to distinguish the full PCC generator from nested or alternative update rules in this setup. Binary actions are a lossy observation of latent belief and can leave substantial ambiguity between model classes.

The action-only ambiguity is not treated as a failure of the experiment. It is an identifiability boundary and motivates richer or repeated decision tasks, varying payoffs, calibrated report models, and joint inference over decision-policy parameters.

## Not established

- general model-class identifiability,
- recovery when received evidence is also latent,
- robustness to misspecified report or action likelihoods,
- human cognitive validity,
- superiority of PCC over other behavioral belief-update models.
